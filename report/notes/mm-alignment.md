# MM / Operator Alignment on RWA-Perp Venues

Research date: 2026-07-11. Focus: is each venue "tightly aligned with an incumbent MM firm" vs "open to all comers"? Confirmed vs rumored flagged per claim; dates given where sources allow.

---

## 1. Hyperliquid HIP-3 builder dexes

Background: HIP-3 ("builder-deployed perpetuals") went live on mainnet 2025-10-13; any deployer staking 500K HYPE (slashable by validator vote) can run its own perp dex on HyperCore, choosing markets, oracle, leverage and fee params.
Sources: [Hyperliquid docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hyperliquid-improvement-proposals-hips/hip-3-builder-deployed-perpetuals), [Datawallet HIP-3 explainer](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/hip-3-explained-hyperliquid-upgrade), [OAK Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/analyses/innovations/what-is-hyperliquid-hip-3-how-it-works-and-use-cases).

Concentration: trade[XYZ] alone is ~90%+ of all HIP-3 open interest and ~96–97% of HIP-3 volume as of Apr–Jun 2026 ([FalconX](https://www.falconx.io/newsroom/the-transformational-potential-of-hyperliquids-hip-3), [KuCoin/Odaily on Felix shutdown, 2026-06-19](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/felix-first-hip-3-platform-on-hyperliquid-announces-shutdown), [FinanceFeeds, 2026-06-15](https://financefeeds.com/ventuals-shuts-down-private-company-perps-on-hyperliquid/)).

### xyz — trade[XYZ] (tradexyz.com / trade.xyz)
- **Operator (confirmed):** perpetual-futures arm of **Unit** (hyperunit.xyz), the main asset-tokenization protocol on Hyperliquid. First HIP-3 deployment, live at HIP-3 activation 2025-10-13. Focus: US equities, indices/ETFs (flagship XYZ100 Nasdaq-style index), commodities, FX, some pre-IPO. Sources: [Datawallet TradeXYZ explainer](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/tradexyz-explained), [trade.xyz docs](https://docs.trade.xyz/).
- **Who quotes the book (confirmed, on-chain analysis dated 2026-04-30):** ChainCatcher wallet-level study identified **Jump Crypto** ($3.15B volume, 2 wallets), **Selini Capital** ($1.03B, 3 wallets), **Wintermute** ($229.6M, funded via OKX), and Polymarket-famous individual **"Powell"** ($4.39B — one of the largest MMs on the venue). Top-5 MMs = 50% of MM volume; top-13 = 80%; top-21 = 90%. Most other MM wallets are unidentifiable but funded from Kraken/Binance/Coinbase/Unit bridges. **No evidence Unit runs its own MM desk** — the study does not attribute any MM wallet to Unit. Source: [ChainCatcher "Who is trading on Trade.xyz?", 2026-04-30](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2261815).
- **Announced MM partnerships:** none found. No press release ties Wintermute/Flowdesk/Auros/Amber to xyz as designated MMs; the firms above appear to quote voluntarily. Wintermute separately quotes ~$199M notional across 76 Hyperliquid markets per an independent analysis repo ([0xLoris](https://github.com/0xLoris/wintermute-hyperliquid-analysis)). **Verdict: open to all comers; multiple independent incumbent MMs quote it without disclosed designation.**

### km / mkts — Markets by Kinetiq
- **Operator (confirmed):** **Kinetiq**, the largest HYPE liquid-staking protocol (~$700M TVL). "Markets" launched ~2026-01-12 as the 5th HIP-3 dex (US500, BABA, EUR/USD, oil, etc.), using Kaiko's HIP-3 oracle and USDH collateral. Kinetiq also runs **Launch**, an "exchange-as-a-service" factory for third parties wanting HIP-3 dexes. Sources: [Phemex, Jan 2026](https://phemex.com/news/article/kinetiq-launches-as-fifth-hip3-dex-on-hyperliquid-47510), [KuCoin flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/kinetiq-transforms-from-lst-protocol-to-exchange-factory-on-hyperliquid), [kinetiq.xyz/launch/markets-by-kinetiq](https://kinetiq.xyz/launch/markets-by-kinetiq).
- **Ticker note:** trackers disagree on the dex code — [Loris/Hyperzap list it as "km"](https://hyperzap.io/hip3/dex/km) while [Hip3Hub lists "mkts" (quote asset mktsUSDC)](https://www.hip3hub.xyz/dexes). Both refer to Markets by Kinetiq; whether "km" and "mkts" are two deployments or one renamed venue is **unresolved** — treat as the same operator.
- **MM relationships (confirmed but unnamed):** In an interview dated 2026-04-17, Kinetiq said it has "strong market maker relationships who have expressed intent to provide robust liquidity on Markets" but **named no firms**; liquidity strategy is demand-driven listings + USDH fee discounts to attract makers. No public LP vault or designated-MM program found. Source: [WEEX interview, 2026-04-17](https://www.weex.com/news/detail/kinetiq-exclusive-interview-from-hyperliquids-largest-lst-protocol-to-the-exchange-factory-302326). Volume is small: ~$3.9M/24h vs xyz's $3.25B ([Hip3Hub, fetched 2026-07-11](https://www.hip3hub.xyz/dexes)). **Verdict: informal/undisclosed MM relationships; effectively open, thin book.**

### vntl — Ventuals (pre-IPO perps) — SHUT DOWN
- **Operator (confirmed):** co-founders Alvin Hsia and Emily Hsia (+ engineer Aris Samad). Pre-IPO perps (OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceX) at up to 10x.
- **Liquidity model (confirmed):** the **Ventuals VLP vault**, operated by **Hyperbeat** "in partnership with the Ventuals team as the de facto source of liquidity" — an HLP-style public vault: anyone deposits stablecoins, vault runs MM strategies on vntl markets and takes a share of trading fees. So liquidity was community-vault, not an incumbent MM firm. Source: [Hyperbeat docs](https://docs.hyperbeat.org/hyperbeat-earn/ventuals-hip-3-vlp-vault).
- **Status (confirmed):** announced wind-down ~2026-06-14/15; froze OpenAI/Anthropic markets at 24h-average prices, returned ~500,000 HYPE (~$33M) to depositors; lifetime volume >$650M. Team hired by **Phantom** (announced shortly after). Sources: [The Defiant wind-down](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/hyperliquid-based-ventuals-winds-down-on-chain-pre-ipo-markets), [FinanceFeeds, 2026-06-15](https://financefeeds.com/ventuals-shuts-down-private-company-perps-on-hyperliquid/), [The Defiant on Phantom hires](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/phantom-hires-ventuals-founders-after-hyperliquid-perps-venue-winds-down).

- **cash — Dreamcash:** mobile-first Hyperliquid trading app by **Supreme Liquid Labs**; HIP-3 markets for TSLA, gold, S&P 500 etc. margined in **USDT0**; **Tether made a strategic investment (Feb 2026, size undisclosed)** and **Selini Capital was tapped as the liquidity provider for its 10 RWA markets** — i.e., a designated MM arrangement. Alive; ~2.75% of HIP-3 volume (second place, early June 2026). Sources: [The Block, Feb 2026](https://www.theblock.co/post/389916/tether-invests-in-hyperliquid-frontend-dreamcash-offering-perps-markets-for-tsla-gold-and-more-using-usdt0-collateral), [MEXC blog](https://blog.mexc.com/news/tether-invests-in-dreamcash-tsla-gold-sp-500-perps-live-on-hyperliquid/), [KuCoin/Odaily volume-share stats](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/felix-first-hip-3-platform-on-hyperliquid-announces-shutdown).
- **para — Paragon:** crypto-native perpetual **index** markets ("tickers you already track" — narrative/index instruments), built by **N8V Labs**, founded by **Taha El-Magbri** (ex-Wall St, ITE Management; sold prior studio to Galaxy Digital in 2025). Went live on Hyperliquid ~2026-04-02. Alive but tiny (~$122K/24h). No MM disclosures found. Sources: [PR Newswire, 2026-04-02](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/paragon-announces-first-crypto-native-perpetual-index-markets-go-live-on-hyperliquid-302733053.html), [Hip3Hub](https://www.hip3hub.xyz/dexes).
- **flx — Felix:** DeFi lending protocol on HyperEVM whose HIP-3 dex traded silver/crude/gold (~$3B volume Dec–Jan) using **USDH** collateral; **announced shutdown 2026-06-19**. Post-mortems blame USDH choice (MMs avoided USDH books once Hyperliquid "Growth Mode" cut USDC fees), late entry vs xyz, and xyz's 95%+ share. Source: [KuCoin/Odaily, 2026-06-19](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/felix-first-hip-3-platform-on-hyperliquid-announces-shutdown).
- **hyna — HyENA:** "internet trading engine with native yield" — **USDe-margined** perp dex built by the **Based** team, **backed by Ethena Foundation** (an investor in Based); launched 2025-12-09. Alignment is with a stablecoin issuer (Ethena), not an MM firm; collateral earns USDe yield. Alive, small (~$2.2M/24h). Sources: [OAK Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/reports/protocols/hyena-ethena-bringing-yield-bearing-collateral-hyperliquid), [Bitget, Dec 2025](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605105707), [Hip3Hub](https://www.hip3hub.xyz/dexes).
- **abcd — ABCDEx:** listed as one of eight HIP-3 dexes on a Dune dashboard-era snapshot ([loris.tools/hip3](https://loris.tools/hip3) search summary), but **no operator, docs, or news found**; absent from current Hip3Hub/Loris live listings as of 2026-07-11. **Status: unverified / likely dormant or defunct.**

### Do HIP-3 deployers run internal MM desks? (conflict-of-interest angle)
- **Structural conflict is real and documented:** the deployer selects AND operates the oracle that sets mark price for liquidations/funding on its own venue; commentary notes weekend oil marks drifting from Monday COMEX opens, and that validator slashing is a backstop that acts only after trader damage. Sources: [Boluo HIP-3 analysis](https://www.boluo.im/crypto/what-is-hip3), [Hyperdash](https://hyperdash.com/learn/hip-3-permissionless-perpetual-markets-on-hyperliquid), [Hyperliquid docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hyperliquid-improvement-proposals-hips/hip-3-builder-deployed-perpetuals).
- **But no confirmed case of a deployer quoting its own book directly.** Observed patterns: xyz = independent firms (Jump/Selini/Wintermute/Powell); Dreamcash = designated external MM (Selini); Ventuals = operator-affiliated **public vault** (VLP via Hyperbeat) doing the market making — the closest thing to "deployer quotes its own book," though with community capital and disclosure. Kinetiq = undisclosed bilateral MM relationships. (Assessment as of 2026-07-11; wallet attribution is inherently incomplete, so an internal desk can't be ruled out — mark as *not evidenced*, not *disproven*.)

## 2. Ostium

- **Trading-firm investors (confirmed):** **$20M Series A co-led by General Catalyst and Jump Crypto, announced 2025-12-03**, with participation from **Coinbase (Ventures), Wintermute Ventures, GSR, Susquehanna (SIG)**, LocalGlobe, Alliance DAO, and angels from Brevan Howard. Total raised >$27M; earlier ~$3.5M round (Balaji Srinivasan, SIG, GSR, Crucible, angels from Bridgewater/Two Sigma). So yes — Jump, Wintermute, GSR, and SIG are all on the cap table. Sources: [BusinessWire, 2025-12-03](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251203478893/en/Ostium-Raises-$20-Million-Series-A-from-General-Catalyst-Jump-Crypto-to-Bring-Global-Markets-Onchain), [CoinLaunch round data](https://coinlaunch.space/events-rounds/ostium-labs-private-round/).
- **Counterparty model (confirmed):** no order book — the **OLP "Market Making Vault"** is the counterparty to all trades. **Anyone can deposit USDC and mint fungible OLP** (open to all comers); LPs earn trading + liquidation fees in exchange for warehousing net trader delta. Sources: [Ostium docs — Market Making Vault](https://ostium-labs.gitbook.io/ostium-docs/shared-liquidity-layer/market-making-vault), [Ostium blog — OLP updates](https://www.ostium.com/blog/olp-updates-a-more-seamless-vault-experience-for-liquidity-providers).
- **External-MM integration (announced, in progress as of late 2025/2026):** Ostium is building a **dynamic hedging engine** that splits settlement from risk-warehousing so **external market makers shoulder directional risk while OLP stays delta-neutral** — i.e., moving from pure vault-counterparty toward vault + professional MM hedgers. No specific MM firm named for this role yet. Sources: [Ostium docs overview](https://ostium-labs.gitbook.io/ostium-docs/ostium-trading-engine/overview), [CoinGecko Ostium explainer](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/what-is-ostium-rwa-crypto).
- **Verdict:** investor-aligned with several incumbent trading firms (Jump/Wintermute/GSR/SIG), but the *liquidity supply itself* is an open public vault, not a designated MM.

## 3. Vest Exchange

- **Backers (confirmed):** seed (Jan 2023) announced with **Jane Street, QCP Capital, Big Brain Holdings**; expanded backing includes **Amber Group, Selini Capital, AscendEX, Infinity Ventures Crypto, Cogitent, Pear VC** and execs from Citadel/BlackRock/HRT; **$5M round March 2025** with largely the same names. Sources: [Vest Medium announcement, 2023](https://medium.com/@VestExchange/announcing-vest-exchange-a-dex-backed-by-jane-street-qcp-capital-and-big-brain-holdings-894592a28b2c), [CMO Intern, Mar 2025](https://www.cmointern.com/2025/03/vest-labs-secures-5-million-seed.html?m=1), [Vest docs](https://docs.vest.exchange/).
- **Model (confirmed):** no CLOB — pricing/risk via **zkRisk**, a ZK-verified unified risk engine; AMM-style execution against an LP pool, "zero trading fees," LPs compensated for risk. Points program for traders and LPs launched 2025-03-13; still running as of Jan 2026; no token yet as of Jan 2026. Sources: [Vest docs](https://docs.vest.exchange/), [CoinLaunch](https://coinlaunch.space/projects/vest-exchange/), [airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/vest/).
- **Status mid-2026 (confirmed alive):** CoinGecko shows **~$25.6M 24h volume, ~$44.6M open interest, 162 pairs** across crypto, US stocks/ETFs (AAPL, MSFT, TSLA, NVDA, SPY, QQQ), commodities and FX; most active pair **SPCX/USDC (SpaceX pre-IPO, ~$4.7M/day)** — though many pairs show no trades in the last 3 hours (thin long tail). Fetched 2026-07-11. Source: [CoinGecko exchange page](https://www.coingecko.com/en/exchanges/vest-exchange-futures).
- **Verdict:** the strongest "aligned with incumbent MM firms" case among these — Jane Street/Amber/QCP/Selini are equity backers of a venue whose model is a house risk-engine + LP pool rather than open competitive quoting. (Whether backers actively trade/hedge on it is **not publicly confirmed**.)

## 4. Aster (stock perps)

- **Backing (confirmed):** grew out of Astherus, seeded by **YZi Labs (ex-Binance Labs) in Nov 2024**; YZi retains a minority position. Widely characterized as Binance/CZ-adjacent. Sources: [Coin Bureau Aster guide](https://coinbureau.com/review/what-is-aster-crypto), [etf.com launch coverage](https://www.etf.com/sections/news/aster-launches-247-stock-perpetual-contracts-trading-exposure-us-equities).
- **Stock perps (confirmed):** 24/7 US-equity perps (AMZN, AAPL, GOOG, META, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA) on Aster Pro, launched 2025 (press release circulated ~July 2025); on 2025-12-10 Aster made NVDA/TSLA stock-perp trading zero-fee — **takers pay 0 but earn no points; makers pay 0 AND earn points**, explicitly to court professional MMs. Sources: [CryptoSlate PR](https://cryptoslate.com/press-releases/aster-launches-24-7-stock-perpetual-contracts-trading-with-exposure-to-u-s-equities/), [Cryptopolitan, Dec 2025](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/aster-dex-eliminate-trading-fees-nvda-tsla/), [Cryptonomist, 2025-12-10](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2025/12/10/aster-stock-perpetuals-zero-fee/).
- **Designated MM program (confirmed, open application):** formal **Market Maker Program** — apply via form, ≥$100M monthly futures volume (on Aster or elsewhere) to qualify, IP whitelisting, tiered preferential fees, hourly maker rebates, and a **~$300K/month reward pool paid in ASTER** by effective maker volume. Sources: [Aster docs — Market Maker Program](https://docs.asterdex.com/program-and-rewards/market-maker-program), [AInvest coverage](https://www.ainvest.com/news/aster-launches-market-maker-program-300-000-monthly-rewards-2505/).
- **Affiliated MMs quoting its equity books:** **not confirmed.** No public disclosure of which firms quote Aster stock perps; given the YZi/Binance lineage, affiliation rumors persist but nothing verifiable was found. **Verdict: formally open-to-all-comers program; opacity about who actually quotes.**

## Bottom-line classification (as of 2026-07-11)

- **Tightly aligned with incumbent trading firms (equity/designated level):** Vest (Jane Street/Amber/QCP/Selini as investors in a house-risk-engine venue); Dreamcash (Selini as designated LP, Tether as investor); Ostium (Jump/Wintermute/GSR/SIG investors, but open vault supplies liquidity).
- **Open to all comers with formal programs:** Aster (public MM program), Ostium OLP, Vest LP pool.
- **Open, liquidity by voluntary incumbent quoting:** trade[XYZ] (Jump, Selini, Wintermute, Powell observed; no disclosed designation).
- **Undisclosed bilateral MM deals:** Markets by Kinetiq.
- **Community-vault model (operator-adjacent MM):** Ventuals VLP (defunct since 2026-06-15).
- **Dead/dormant:** Ventuals (2026-06-15), Felix (2026-06-19), ABCDEx (unverified, likely dormant).
- **Structural note:** every HIP-3 deployer controls its own oracle, so even without an internal desk the deployer-sets-oracle conflict stands; no confirmed instance of a deployer quoting its own book with proprietary capital was found, but wallet attribution is incomplete.
